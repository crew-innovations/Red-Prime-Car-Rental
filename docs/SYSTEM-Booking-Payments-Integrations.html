Booking, payments and integrations

RED PRIME RENTALS. What the program does, read from the code. Checked on the live CRM on 26 November 2026. Nothing on Integrations is Connected yet.

Read this first

Three jobs are separate. Booking saves a request. A payment link collects money later. Integrations is where the keys live. Pasting a key does not switch a job on. The card must be switched on, and Test connection must pass, before payments, lead ads and chat use it. Google Maps prices and the document reader start when the key is saved and the switch is on, even if the test has not passed.

On a car page, before dates, the panel shows “Security deposit: AED …” or “Security deposit: —”. A trip and a package never show a deposit line. The deposit is the admin field on the car or yacht. There is no second field called extra security deposit. Ticked add-ons are inside the total.

The email after Book now prints “Total: AED [booking total]” and “Our team will contact you shortly and send you the payment link.” It does not print the deposit and it does not name Stripe, Tabby or Tamara.

The booking’s security-deposit field is stored as 0. The same amount is kept inside the price calculation. Staff see it in Security deposit when they change the car on the booking, or when the price form fills a still-empty deposit.

The lead after a car booking

Car pipeline: Payment Pending
→
Create quotation hidden
→
Stays open until the booking is Confirmed

A yacht, trip or package uses the yacht pipeline. Waiting for payment moves that lead to Deposit Pending, not Payment Pending. Create quotation is hidden while a live booking exists. A live booking is any booking that is not cancelled and not an inquiry.

A second Book now from the same phone and the same product within 10 minutes does not make a second lead. If that lead has no live booking, the booking is created on it. If a live booking already exists, the same booking is returned.

→

Money recorded once

Opening the thank-you page does not record money. A mismatched amount is not recorded. Status becomes Needs checking. The note starts “Paid [currency] [amount] but [asked] was asked”.

While the stored status is still Waiting for payment, and the payment status is Paid or the advance is reached, the booking label shown to staff is Paid, documents pending. The stored status does not change. A car is not confirmed by the payment. Staff confirm after the documents are approved. If the advance-confirm switch is on and the documents are already complete, the existing automatic confirm still runs.

The customer message after payment, when documents are still missing, asks them to upload documents in their account and says the booking is not confirmed yet.

No browser key adds “no browser key: map preview off”. A wrong browser key still passes. The server key is sent with no website address, so restrict it by the Hostinger IP. The browser key must allow https://redprimerentals.com/*, https://www.redprimerentals.com/* and https://crm.redprimerentals.com/*. The live CRM is on redprimerentals.com.

A failed route on the public site is shown as “We could not find a road route for these addresses. Please check them.” The Google sentence is kept on the card when Google answers 401 or 403.

TikTok and Snapchat

Shared secret saved and switched on
→
Platform posts one signed lead
→
Then Test connection can pass

The CRM does not call TikTok or Snapchat. Test connection only looks for a lead that already arrived and was accepted. Until then: “No signed test lead received yet.” Addresses: /api/webhooks/leads/tiktok and /api/webhooks/leads/snapchat. Signature is HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body. Headers: X-TikTok-Signature and X-Snap-Signature. The body needs a lead id. No secret, or the switch off, answers 404 and saves nothing. A bad signature answers 400 and the text “[header] does not match”. Today both cards are on, the secret is missing, and no lead has arrived.

Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp

One card. Address /api/webhooks/messages/meta. Meta’s address check fails until the verify token saved in the CRM is the same text typed in Meta. That is the failure already stored today. The test also needs a Page token or a WhatsApp token. WhatsApp also needs the phone number ID. Replies use those tokens. A customer message becomes a lead. The agent continues it in Messages.

Document reader

One API key. The test sends a sentence and looks for the word “true” in the answer. Staff still check the reading before it is saved. The reader runs when the key is saved and the switch is on. It does not wait for Connected.

9. Order that matches the code

  1. Booking on the website never shows a payment method. Do not look for one there.
  2. Car and yacht prices and the refundable deposit show on the estimate. The deposit is not inside the total. The saved booking field for the deposit stays 0 until staff set it.
  3. Limousine with Maps off stays “price on request”. With the agent-confirm switch on, the ride is an inquiry.
  4. Paste one integration’s keys. Save. Do that provider’s webhook step. Press Test connection. Read the sentence on the card.
  5. Only a Connected payment card adds its text button on the pay link.
  6. Send one test lead from TikTok or Snapchat before expecting Test connection to pass.
  7. Confirm the booking by hand after documents are approved. Payment alone does not confirm a car.
