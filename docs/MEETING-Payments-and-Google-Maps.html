Payments and Google Maps

RED PRIME RENTALS · What the program does · Live CRM checked 26 November 2026 at https://redprimerentals.com/crm · No API key is written here.

On the website, while the customer is booking, no payment method is shown. Stripe, Tabby, Tamara, Paymob and PayPal are not on the car page, the yacht page, the trip page, the limousine page, or the enquiry form. Connecting them in Settings › Integrations does not add them there. The Book now form has no card button and no instalment button.

Those names appear only later, on https://redprimerentals.com/pay/…, after a staff member sends a payment link, or after the customer presses “Pay the balance” or “Pay the advance” in their account. The buttons on that page are the five text buttons in section 1. Only gateways whose card says Connected are in the list.

The car price is shown when the customer picks dates. The security deposit saved on the car by the admin is shown as its own line, “Refundable deposit”. It is not added into “Estimated total”. The same deposit line is shown for a yacht that has a deposit. A trip, a package and a limousine do not show a deposit line. The email after Book now prints the booking total and does not print the deposit. The booking saved from the website stores the rental total. Its security-deposit field stays 0 until a staff member sets it. The deposit amount is kept inside the price calculation on that booking.

Website flows

Every public form does one of two things. A form marked enquiry creates a lead only. A form marked quote sends request_type: booking. Neither form takes a card.

1. Enquiry and the quick-quote popup

Contact pages and the popup use form[data-enquiry]. Submit goes to /api/v1/enquiries. The program creates a lead. It does not create a booking, does not show a price total, and does not show a payment method. The customer sees the success state of that form and a reference.

2. Car — Book now

The car page shows the admin’s daily price in the panel (“from AED … per day”), or “Price on request” when that rate is empty. Before dates are chosen the panel also shows “Security deposit: AED [amount]” when the car’s Security deposit field is filled, or “Security deposit: —” when it is empty. The same amount is in the car facts when it is filled.

After pickup and return are chosen, the page calls /api/v1/price-estimate. The answer is drawn as:

  • “Available for your dates”, or “Not available for these dates.”, or “Availability confirmed by our team”.
  • The rental line, then each extra or delivery line.
  • “Subtotal” and “VAT [rate]%” when VAT is on and not included. When VAT is included, a note “incl. VAT [rate]%”.
  • “Estimated total”. This total is rental + extras − discount + VAT. The deposit is not in this number.
  • “Refundable deposit” and the car’s deposit, only when that amount is above 0.
  • “Estimate only. Nothing is booked or charged yet. Our team confirms availability and the final price.”

Ticked add-ons are lines inside the total. They are not the security deposit. The admin field on the car is labelled “Security deposit”. There is no second field called “extra security deposit”.

Book now then asks for name, mobile and email. Under the button: “Nothing is charged now. Our team sends you a payment link.” Submit goes to /api/v1/booking-requests with request_type: booking. When the price exists, the program saves a booking with status Pending payment, holds the car for the hours in Business settings (default 24), and emails the customer. The email lines are: the booking title, the dates, “Total: AED [booking total]”, “Our team will contact you shortly and send you the payment link.”, and the hold sentence when a hold is set. The email does not name a gateway and does not print the deposit.

On screen the customer then sees “Booking received” and “Your booking [ref] is saved and waiting for payment. We sent the details and your account login to your email. Our team will send you the payment link shortly.” Plus “We keep it for you for [hours] hours.”

The website does not copy the car deposit into the booking’s security-deposit field. That field is stored as 0. The price calculation saved on the booking does include deposit equal to the car’s amount. In the CRM, changing the car on the booking form writes that amount into Security deposit. The price form also writes it when the field is still 0 and the calculation has a deposit.

If the engine has no price, no booking is created. The lead stays, and the customer sees the request-received text, not “waiting for payment”.

3. Yacht — Book now

Same steps as the car: dates, then the estimate, then name, phone and email, then Book now. “Refundable deposit” is shown when the yacht’s deposit is above 0, on its own line, not inside the total. The yacht page does not print the deposit in the side panel before dates are chosen. The booking’s security-deposit field is stored as 0, the same as a car.

4. Trip and package — Book now

The page shows the published price, or “Price on request”. The estimate uses the same total lines. deposit in the price engine is only for a car or a yacht, so the deposit row is not shown. A trip needs an available date. A package that uses departures needs a bookable departure. Book now creates a Pending payment booking when a price exists, and holds the seats. No payment method is on the page.

5. Limousine — Book now

The customer picks a class, pickup, destination, date and time. The page calls the transfer quote. A distance ride with Google Maps connected shows kilometres, minutes and a total on the class cards. Without a server key the quote returns no price and the note “Price on request. Our team will send you a quotation.” The transfer quote does not send security_deposit, so no deposit line is drawn.

Submit still uses request_type: booking. When the business setting says an agent confirms the limousine price, the booking is saved as Inquiry, nothing is held, and the screen says “Ride request received” and “Your request [ref] is with our team. We confirm the final price shortly, then you accept and pay on your ride page.” The API message is the same. No gateway is listed on that page.

6. After the booking exists

  1. Staff see the booking as Waiting for payment, unless it is a limousine inquiry. The lead sits on Payment Pending. Create quotation is hidden while that booking is live.
  2. Staff press Payment link and choose advance, balance, or refundable security deposit, and the amount. The link does not name a gateway.
  3. The customer opens the link and sees every Connected gateway as the text buttons in section 1. Nothing is charged on the website booking form.
  4. In the customer account, “Pay the advance (AED …)” and “Pay the balance (AED …)” open that same page. They are absent while no gateway is Connected.
  5. If the hold ends unpaid, the booking is cancelled and the lead returns to Contacted with the note “Website hold ended, not paid” and a follow-up for today.

1. The pay page, word for word

Logos. The program does not show a Stripe, Tabby, Tamara, Paymob or PayPal logo. There is no image file for those brands. The website text does not name them. It says “Pay safely with a secure payment link” and “Once your booking is confirmed we send you a secure online payment link.”

The payment link does not name the gateway. The address is https://redprimerentals.com/pay/…. WhatsApp is sent with this exact sentence: “Payment for [booking or quote number]: AED [amount] · [that address]”. The gateway name is not in that sentence. The customer opens the page and then sees the buttons below. The customer account button is “Pay the balance (AED …)” or “Pay the advance (AED …)”. Those words do not include Stripe, Tabby or Tamara. An open link already made shows “Pay AED [amount] online · [Advance to confirm / Balance / Refundable security deposit]”.

Staff see the gateway in Accounts › Online payments (column Gateway) and in the payment description: “Online payment · Stripe · PR-00001 · [booking]”.

2. When a pay button is shown

A gateway is offered only when all of these are true: Switched on, every required key is saved, and the card status is Connected. Connected is set only by a successful Test connection.

Pressing Save does not set Connected. Save sets “Details saved, not tested” when the required keys are filled, or “Not set up” when one is still empty. In that state the customer has no pay button. Staff who press Payment link get: “No payment gateway is connected yet (Settings › Integrations).”

Empty tick-boxes on the payment link mean every Connected gateway is offered. Ticked boxes limit the page to those. The customer account always offers every Connected gateway. It does not let the customer pick before the pay page.

The pay buttons on a booking are inside the price card. That card is shown only when the customer account is allowed to see payments. With no Connected gateway, onlinePayment() returns no amount and no buttons.

Maps does not wait for Connected

Payments wait for status Connected. Maps does not. isReady() is true when the card is switched on and the server key is saved. The public limousine page then calls Google, including before Test connection.

A failed test sets status to Error and does not switch the card off. The next price request calls Google again.

The public “On map” button is added only when the server key is saved and a browser key is saved. Live drivers and the customer track map use the browser key whenever the card is switched on, even if the server key is missing.

With no server key, a distance ride on the website returns price empty and the note “Price on request. Our team will send you a quotation.” Hourly hire does not call Routes. In the CRM the distance box says “Google Maps is not set up: type the road distance.” Calculate route is disabled. On a lead, Quick limousine price says “No price yet: Google Maps is not connected (Settings › Integrations), so the road distance is unknown.” and still offers “Send booking link on WhatsApp”.

What Test connection checks, and what it does not

It checks the server key with one Routes call. Success text: “Routes API working ([n] km test route)”. If the browser key box is empty it adds “ · no browser key: map preview off”. If a browser key is saved, the test does not call Google with it. A wrong browser key still produces a successful test.

The test does not call Places, Geocoding, Maps Embed or Maps JavaScript.

Places and Geocoding failures return no rows and no error on the form. The address box still accepts what was typed. The map script’s load error sets the pin address line to “—”.

On the public site, every failed route (bad key, API disabled, no road) is replaced with one sentence: “We could not find a road route for these addresses. Please check them.” The Google sentence is kept on the Integrations card only when Google answers 401 or 403. That also sets Last error and status Error. Other failures are under Recent activity as “Google Maps error: [Google’s message]” or one of the sentences below.

The server sends the key in the header X-Goog-Api-Key. It does not send a website address. A server key restricted in Google to “HTTP referrers” is refused by Google, and the test shows “Google Maps error:” plus Google’s sentence. Restrict that key by IP, to the Hostinger server IP, and to Routes API, Places API (New) and Geocoding API.

The browser key is in the page, so Google’s referrer check applies. The live CRM and the public site both use https://redprimerentals.com. Add these referrers on the browser key: https://redprimerentals.com/*, https://www.redprimerentals.com/*, https://crm.redprimerentals.com/*. Limit that key to Maps Embed API and Maps JavaScript API. A browser key limited only to crm.redprimerentals.com does not match the pages that are open today.

Country code on the card is sent as regionCode and as Places includedRegionCodes. The field help says two letters, for example ae. A wrong code limits the address list to that country. It does not stop the Routes test between Dubai Mall and the airport.

A successful route is reused for 30 minutes. A geocode result is reused for 1 day. A failed call is not stored in that cache. Kilometres are rounded with Business settings “Road distance rounding”. The default in code is 0.1 km (28.64 becomes 28.6).

6. Do this on their computer, in this order

  1. Sign in to the CRM as Admin. Open Settings › Integrations. Confirm the table in section 3 before pasting anything.
  2. On the client’s Google account, open their project. Billing has to be on or Google returns the “API has not been used or it is disabled” class of error. Enable five APIs: Routes, Places API (New), Geocoding, Maps Embed, Maps JavaScript.
  3. Create the server key. IP restriction: Hostinger server IP. API restriction: Routes, Places API (New), Geocoding.
  4. Create the browser key. Referrers listed above. API restriction: Maps Embed and Maps JavaScript.
  5. Google Maps › Set up. Switched on. Country code ae. Paste the server key. Paste the browser key. Save. The toast is “Google Maps saved” and, when the server key is present, “Now press Test connection.”
  6. Press Test connection. The pass sentence is “Routes API working ([n] km test route)”. Then open a lead › Quick limousine price, type a pickup and a drop-off of at least 3 letters, and read the kilometres under the class list. Then open the public limousine page and press On map.
  7. Payments, one card at a time. Leave Mode on Test. Paste the keys. Save. Press Test connection. Read the sentence in section 4. Do not switch Mode to Live in this meeting. Live charges real money. The helper text on the switch says that.
  8. After one card says Connected, open a test booking and press Payment link. Open the link. The buttons on the page are the list in section 1, and only for gateways that are Connected. The WhatsApp sentence does not name the gateway.

Use the client’s Google account and the client’s payment accounts. The project number 115481714969 in the October error is the project of a key that was tried before. It is not a project to reuse unless they confirm it is theirs and Routes is enabled on it.

7. What stays the same

  • No new status, price, deposit, VAT figure, or document rule is changed by pasting these keys.
  • A card payment does not confirm a car by itself. The confirm rules already in the CRM still apply.
  • The website does not charge a card when the customer presses Book now. Staff send the payment link, or the customer presses Pay the balance / Pay the advance in the account after a gateway is Connected.
  • An enquiry is a lead. It does not create a payment link.
